# Fake Doubler  
*(aka Raddoppio Azzoppato)*

## Players and Deck
Players: 2–5

Short deck:
- 8–Ace (28 cards): 2–3 players
- 7–Ace (32 cards): 4 players
- 6–Ace (36 cards): 5 players

Each player forms a 5-card hand.  
One shared **central card** is in play.

---

## Dealing and Flow

### Initial Deal
Each player receives:
- 2 face-down cards
- 1 face-up card

After all player cards have been dealt, the dealer reveals the **central card as the final step of the initial deal**.  
The central card remains visible for the entire hand.

First betting round.

---

### Face-Up Cards
The dealer then deals:
- a second face-up card → betting round
- a third face-up card → betting round

Betting order is determined by the **highest visible hand**  
(straight or flush projects do not count).

---

### Reveal of a Hidden Card
After the betting round following the last dealt card and **before the final betting round**:

- each player reveals **one** of their face-down cards
- the choice of which card to reveal is up to the player
- reveals occur **in betting order**, starting from the player with the **highest visible hand**

One face-down card always remains hidden until showdown.

---

### Final Bet and Showdown
After the reveal round:
- final betting round
- showdown

---

## The Central Card

- The central card is shared by all players and may be considered a sixth card.
- If a player holds a card of the same rank as the central card, the central card is **completely unusable** for that player (*disabled*).
- If the player does not hold that rank, they may apply **one** of the following bonuses:
  - **double the rank** (pair-based hands only)
  - **change the suit** (for flush-based hands)

The central card is used only if it produces a stronger hand.  
It can never worsen a hand.

---

## Pair-Based Hands

If the player is **not disabled**, the central card allows only:

- high card may form a pair (via doubling)
- pair → two pair
- two pair → possible internal improvement
- trips → full house

No other category jumps are possible.

If the player is **disabled**, the central card cannot be used in any way.

---

## Straights

- The central card never doubles.
- If not disabled, it may **complete an incomplete straight** if it provides the missing rank.
- If not disabled, it may **improve the rank of an existing straight**
  (e.g. K-high straight → A-high straight).
- **The central card is not a wild card for straights.**

---

## Flushes and Straight Flushes

- The central card never doubles.
- If not disabled, **its suit may be changed to complete a flush or straight flush**,
  provided it is the only missing card.
- The central card never multiplies hands.
- It cannot create four of a kind and cannot promote a made straight or flush beyond its category.

---

## Design Principle

> **The central card accentuates, it does not replace, the strength of the hand.**
